FNF Animation Injector

Turn any Roblox game into a rap battle. Your note keys play poses, an FNF arrow strip lights up, just like the original game, and a cinematic camera follows you and your opponent, either for recording use or an organized rap battle. It also has a health bar, a boss battle view, split screen and silhouette mode.
